Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, including your email address and password. We also collect the financial data you input into the tracker, such as expense amounts, categories, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. We use your information to maintain your account, generate your spending reports, and improve the user experience of Spendly.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal and financial information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers that compromise your privacy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
